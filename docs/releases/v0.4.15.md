# Super Metroid 0.4.15

Optional Dread shoulders hold Samus's feet in place while aiming through the
original cannon directions. Airborne aiming preserves horizontal momentum;
releasing the shoulder restores steering and permits another jump. Ground Down
aims diagonally down, while airborne aiming also supports straight down.
Custom right-stick positions assign each weapon a unique direction and retain
saved choices when the custom layout is disabled. Falling down-aim turning is
available as a separate control option.

Zero Mission shoulders retain missile controls through the pause-menu fade.
Quick Power Bomb requests wait for native weapon selection before firing.
Post-wall-jump spin artwork follows the selected spin-animation mod directly
after the foot frames. The laser follows cannon movement and passes behind
opaque sprites, including shots and the cannon tip.

Optional immediate door passage removes the remaining physical cap delay after
the native opening requirement succeeds. Continuous upward arrivals keep the
hatch clear until Samus's whole body has entered. Extras now exposes neighboring
room gameplay, continuous transitions and doorway fade separately.
Room metadata is restored consistently when reusing loaded resources.
Varia and Gravity Suit acquisition effects expand across the widescreen view,
including room edges and neighboring scenery.

Optional mods stop the Morph Ball's rolling animation while stationary,
preserve spin protection against Fire Geysers, and exchange the Spring Ball and
X-ray locations. The item swap retains native collection flags and the remaining
pickup in partially collected saves. Map statistics and maximum ammo share the
bottom corners without covering the map controls.
