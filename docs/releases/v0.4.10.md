# Super Metroid 0.4.10

With Neighboring Room Logic enabled, pirate lasers now keep animating and
moving when a Continuous room becomes the active room. The room's native
projectile processor starts with its normal state, and later suspension by
the game still works.

The Kraid, repeat save-station and upward-hatch fixes from 0.4.9 are retained.
Other Continuous Rooms and sound reports remain under investigation.
