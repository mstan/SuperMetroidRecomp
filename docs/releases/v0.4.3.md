Super Metroid v0.4.3 opens the widescreen view across connected rooms and secret
passages, and adds continuous movement through ordinary doors.

- Show neighboring rooms with their own terrain, palettes, items and animated
  environmental effects, including lava and Ceres steam.
- Keep neighboring enemies active by default. **Mods > Widescreen > Neighboring
  room logic** can turn that activity Off while preserving scenery and doors.
- Use continuous room transitions by default. Choose **Native** under
  **Room transitions** to retain the original door animation.
- Open door bubbles from either side with the required native weapons and
  ammunition. Closed doors and walls continue to block interactions.
- Preserve resident enemies, projectiles, collected items and door state when
  entering and revisiting rooms. Boss encounters and progression still start
  through their normal room-entry conditions.

Fresh installs enable Widescreen with adaptive Fit. Existing saved aspect,
renderer and mod choices remain honored. Keep your settings and native SRAM
saves when updating; save states belong to their exact build.

Supply your own supported unmodified Super Metroid ROM. Packages contain the
precompiled runtime and mod catalog, with no ROMs, developer saves or toolchain.
