# Super Metroid 0.4.13

Continuous Rooms preserves native shutter and boss-door ownership during room
entry. Connected scenery and paired hatch openings align at the Crocomire
save-room connection, while its original door transitions remain in control.

The weapon wheel restores the previous beam selection when it closes. The
Controls page also provides a separate Fire missile action without changing the
selected weapon or its HUD indicator.

Diagnostics recording includes bounded audio delivery counters, frame windows
and the active audio and renderer context before shutdown. The visible
Diagnostics recording mod remains enabled by default and respects its saved
off setting. These additions improve diagnostic reports; they do not establish
a fix for device-specific crackling.
