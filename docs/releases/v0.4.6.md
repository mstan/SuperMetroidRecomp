# Super Metroid 0.4.6

Widescreen rendering now preserves the native ownership of cinematic sprites,
Mother Brain's tank head, station dialogue HUD rows and the escape message
canvas. Power Bombs keep their black subscreen behind liquid boundaries, and
adjacent-room graphics promotion preserves shared escape timer tiles.
Elevator side walls remain visible through the departing room's native fade.

OpenGL framebuffer sampling clamps at the image edges, including after a shader
preset is disabled. Linear scaling no longer blends opposite edge pixels.
Active presets retain their own addressing rules.

Extras scrolls with the selected row, restores that selection after a current
snapshot load, and can return through normal save/configuration shutdown to the
launcher. Muted and shortened low-health warnings retain their selected policy
after a station is used. Continuous Rooms is labeled experimental.

New optional mods provide persistent native button assignments, Moonwalk and
Auto Cancel across game files; independent incoming and outgoing damage and
enemy/boss health settings; modest beam and missile speed adjustments; and
Space Jump artwork for ordinary spin jumps. Additional air jumps still require
the equipped Space Jump ability. Health and projectile settings retain native
limits and collision behavior.

Adaptive Fit remains the fresh default. Existing settings and native saves
are preserved. Host snapshots remain specific to their runtime build.
