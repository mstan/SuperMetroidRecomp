Super Metroid v0.4.4 adds a Connected rooms choice to the launcher and native
pause Options. Turn it Off to keep widescreen within the current room with
original room gameplay and door transitions. Turn it On to use connected
scenery and the saved neighboring-room and transition options.

Connected dry rooms now initialize with native absent-liquid values, preserving
normal ground movement when entered. Water and lava keep their selected native
surfaces.

Fresh installs use adaptive widescreen Fit. Existing saved aspect and mod
choices remain honored. Keep your settings and native SRAM saves when updating;
save states belong to their exact build.

Supply your own supported unmodified Super Metroid ROM. Packages contain the
precompiled runtime and mod catalog, with no ROMs, developer saves or toolchain.
