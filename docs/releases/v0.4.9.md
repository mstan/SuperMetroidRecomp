# Super Metroid 0.4.9

Continuous Rooms now leaves Kraid and his dependent enemy parts under the
native encounter loader, preventing a neighboring preview from running those
parts without their boss. The approach and eye doorway work without High Jump.

Save stations can be used again after leaving and returning through a
Continuous room transition. The native save prompt and SRAM writer handle
each save. Upward Continuous door travel now carries Samus through the whole
arrival frame before returning control, so releasing Jump does not strand her
below the upper room's floor.

This release includes the existing doorway lighting choices, Ceres lighting,
local gameplay diagnostics and saved control options from 0.4.8. Continuous
Rooms remains experimental; other scripted encounters, transitions and audio
reports are still being investigated.
