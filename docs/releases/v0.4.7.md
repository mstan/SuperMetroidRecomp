# Super Metroid 0.4.7

The **Doorway fade** dropdown under **Mods → Continuous Rooms** offers Room
lighting blend, Doorway shadow, and No fade. Room lighting blend is the default.
The choice is saved and applies to seamless crossings.

Continuous Rooms preserves the Wrecked Ship eye door's collision and lets Samus
clear the open cap after returning to the Basement. Widescreen terrain and
liquid extend through the affected shaft and cavern margins, and colored lights
stay aligned during the Ceres escape. Shutter segments move smoothly at higher
presentation rates.

**Gameplay Diagnostics** is visible in Mods and records local replay diagnostics
by default. Players can turn recording off independently of presentation mods.
Adaptive Fit remains the fresh widescreen default. Saved preferences and native
saves carry forward.
