Super Metroid v0.4.5 separates Widescreen and Continuous Rooms into independent
launcher mods. Widescreen expands the current room. Continuous Rooms exposes
adjoining scenery and offers neighboring-room logic and Continuous/Native door
transitions, including at 4:3. Continuous Rooms is experimental and starts Off
on fresh installs; Widescreen still starts On with adaptive Fit. Existing saved
choices retain their effective behavior when migrating to the separate switches.

Optional quality-of-life features:

- **Low-health alarm:** choose Shortened or Muted, or leave the mod disabled for
  the original warning. Health indicators and other sound effects remain enabled.
- **Zero Mission shoulder controls:** the missile shoulder selects power bombs
  in Morph Ball using the selected hold or toggle behavior. Native firing and
  ammunition consumption still apply.
- **Pause readouts:** play time/item percentage and maximum ammo occupy the
  lower left and right corners. Each readout remains independently optional.
- **One-missile red doors:** open red doors with one standard missile.
- **Run by default (hold to walk):** invert the configured Run button during
  gameplay while retaining normal menu controls.

Elective enhancements remain disabled by default. Preserve settings and native
SRAM when updating; execution snapshots are specific to their runtime build.
