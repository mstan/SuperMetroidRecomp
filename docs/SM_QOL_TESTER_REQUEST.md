This is my personal favorite for this project. This romhack has always felt like a perfect remaster to me. The pixel work updates are nice. The QoL added routes between areas was nice. It had Respin and Momentum saving
I think this was the one where you could move the ShineSpark up and down slightly as you moved. It is a really cool project and it's been on my mind if we could feed it through this process somehow

These are the types of things I had in mind:

- INTRO SKIP/CREDITS SKIP
- Time counter in menu
- Change one shoulder button into Zero/Fusion diagonal aim, and other shoulder into missile hold/toggle
- Hold down in morphball with bomb, cancel bomb jump
- Red laser pointer where shots will go when aiming with shoulder button. I.e. Metroid Prime Origins aiming
- Zero mission styled map - door beam icons, maybe higher density to the checkered dot matrix background. Just to make it easier to see and understand where the one ways and doors are
- Show whole map like in Symphony of the Night, zoom in and out
- Show total missile count in pause menu? HUD?
- Fix falling shooting down directional controls. They always felt backwards to me
- Right stick missile weapons radial menu?
- Missile Refill fills supers too
- Screw attack should be able to break beam breakable blocks. C'mon now
- Fix space jump limiters
- Morph Ball as long-hold down toggle
- Speedboost in ball form toggle, hold sprint
- Maintain momentum toggle
- Charge beam magnet for health and ammo
- Faster elevators
- Ability to go back to game main menu from mission complete screen without resetting
- Increase bomb amount toggle
- Toggle shinespark healthdrain
- Cut door transitions as much as possible
- Ability to leave Tourian before Mother Brain battle. That was a save lock in the original game and that sucks
- Auto charge toggle
- Reduce screenshake toggle
- Respin toggle
- Hold down on ground to enter morph ball
- Shinespark able to be moved up and down/left and right slightly while flying
- Mini map on screen toggle with or without pausing game in background (like in Diablo)
- Toggles to 1.)cut item pickup jingle and 2.)instant item pick up (no game pause on pickup)(maybe could work for SAVE COMPLETED. too?)

Not all of these are congruous but this list was more being jotted down while I played.
