# Runtime notices

The launcher uses recomp-ui and Dear ImGui, with the licenses alongside this
file. The font and image notices identify the bundled LatoLatin, Noto and
OpenMoji assets. The font files are unchanged copies from the pinned recomp-ui
revision; their accompanying license texts are included under `fonts/`.

Sources: https://github.com/RetroPortingToolKit/recomp-ui and
https://github.com/ocornut/imgui .
